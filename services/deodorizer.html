Yard Deodorizer Service

Even a clean yard can harbor invisible odors. Our pet-safe deodorizer treatment eliminates odors at the source, leaving your yard fresh, clean, and inviting.

What Is the Yard Deodorizer Treatment?

Our yard deodorizer is a professional-grade, pet-safe treatment that neutralizes the bacteria and enzymes responsible for lingering pet waste odors. Unlike air fresheners that mask smells, our formula breaks down odor compounds at the molecular level.

Applied after your regular waste removal visit, the treatment is safe for pets and children and leaves no harmful residues. The result is a yard that smells as clean as it looks.

Benefits of Yard Deodorizer Service

Eliminates Odors at the Source

Our formula targets and neutralizes the bacteria that cause pet waste odors — not just masking them, but removing them entirely.

100% Pet & Child Safe

The treatment is non-toxic, pet-safe, and child-safe. Pets can re-enter the yard as soon as the treatment dries.

Long-Lasting Results

Each treatment provides extended odor control, especially when paired with regular waste removal service visits.

Protects Your Lawn

Residual pet waste enzymes can damage grass and soil over time. Our deodorizer neutralizes these compounds, helping keep your lawn healthy and green.
